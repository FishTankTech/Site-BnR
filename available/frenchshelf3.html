Synopsis

The nine heads of this work represent portraits by Robert Cremean of his immediate family of which (At the time of creation) he was the only surviving member.

As of December 2022, and at the age of ninety years, he declared he viewed the above portraits a fair representation of his family, and saw no reason as to why that would change.

His fraternal grandfather is on the far left followed by his fraternal grandmother and his maternal grandfather.

The single portrait in gray is that of his maternal grandmother and is followed by a representation of his eldest sister, of himself and of his youngest sister. These three are followed by his parents, the representation of his father on the left and his mother on the right.
